What's in Marietta's map

  • Every street from OpenStreetMap, with the one-ways, signals and turn costs that routes follow.
  • House numbers from public address records, estimated from public address ranges where a street has no address points.
  • Fire stations from the department's own list where we have it. Otherwise they're drafted from OpenStreetMap and shown as estimates until we have the Marietta Fire Department's list.
  • First-due areas from the department's own districts where we have them, and otherwise estimated by drive time from each station.
  • Target hazards from OpenStreetMap: hospitals, schools, senior living, big complexes and venues.

Questions

StreetDrill in Marietta

Does StreetDrill work in Marietta?

Yes. If Marietta is already on StreetDrill's list, it downloads right away. If it isn't, type Marietta, GA during setup or in Profile under City: StreetDrill builds it from open map data in 10 to 30 minutes and adds it to the list for everyone. Then pick your station and study its first-due area, a radius around it, or the whole city.

Are Marietta's fire stations in it?

Yes. The Marietta Fire Department runs six stations, 51 through 56. Where we have a department's own station list or districts, StreetDrill uses them. Otherwise, stations are drafted from OpenStreetMap and shown as estimates until the department's list is added, and first-due areas are estimated by drive time from each station. Send us the Marietta Fire Department's station list and we'll make them exact.

Is every Marietta address in the city?

No. Many Marietta mailing addresses are in unincorporated Cobb County, outside the city limits. StreetDrill builds Marietta to the city limits, so you drill the streets inside them.

Is StreetDrill made by the Marietta Fire Department?

No. StreetDrill is an independent study aid made by Hantis USA, LLC. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Marietta Fire Department, the City of Marietta or any other agency. The Marietta Fire Department's maps, CAD and SOGs come first, so verify routes before relying on them.

Can I study another city too?

Yes. Working a trade or overtime, or testing somewhere new? Switch cities in Profile under City. Type in Kennesaw, Smyrna or any other city nearby, and it's built the same way. Each city keeps its own progress and home base, so switching back to Marietta picks up where you left off.

Does it need a signal?

Only to download Marietta. After that, its streets, addresses, stations and target hazards are on your phone, so every game works offline, in quarters or on the rig.
