Support

Answers to common questions, and a real person at the other end of the email.

Email us

support@streetdrill.app. Tell us your phone (iPhone or Android, and the model), which game, and what you were doing, and include screenshots if you can. For a route or address that looks wrong, send the address.

Getting started

How do I get StreetDrill?

StreetDrill is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone, and to Google Play for Android, in the United States. Email support@streetdrill.app and we'll tell you the day it's out. The first 100 people to sign in with Apple or Google in the app get lifetime access free.

How do I sign in?

Type your name and tap Start studying, or use Continue with Apple (iPhone and iPad) or Continue with Google. Either way, your progress stays on that phone: signing in tells people apart on a shared phone, and checks whether your account gets StreetDrill free. Beta testers can tap I have a beta tester invite and enter the Tester ID and access code from their invite. If it says the code doesn't match, check the Tester ID first: it looks like jane-doe.

How do I pick my city and station?

When you first start, StreetDrill asks Where do you work? Type your city and state, or tap Use my location, then pick your station. Change them any time in Profile: City for the city, Home base for your station or any address, and Study area for that station's first-due district, a radius around your home base, or the whole city.

What do the difficulty levels do?
  • Easy: main roads first, with extra hints, like street names on the map in route games.
  • Standard: a blank map with no street names. Neighborhood streets included.
  • Expert: every street, fewer hints, and no map until you answer. Like the written test.

Change it in Profile under Difficulty.

Buying StreetDrill

What does it cost?

$9.99, once. StreetDrill is free to download and setup is free; a one-time purchase in the app unlocks every game and every city for life, with every future update. No subscription. The purchase belongs to the Apple or Google account you buy it with, so Restore purchases brings it back on a new phone from the same store. The first 100 people to sign in with Apple or Google in the app get lifetime access free.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the Unlock screen, tap Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple account (iPhone and iPad) or Google account (Android) you bought it with. Each store keeps its own purchases, so a purchase on iPhone doesn't carry over to Android, or the other way round.

How do I get a refund?

The store that took the payment handles refunds. On iPhone and iPad, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your Google Play order history.

Does a station iPad need a purchase for each person?

No. The unlock belongs to the phone or iPad, so one purchase on a station iPad works for everyone who studies on it, each with their own progress.

Your city

My city isn't on the list.

Type it in anyway, as city and state. StreetDrill builds any US city from open map data and adds it to the list for everyone. It takes 10 to 30 minutes: keep the app open and it moves onto your city when it's ready. If a build fails, the app says why, and you can try again later. To keep the builder running for everyone, each internet connection can ask for three new cities a day.

Why do some stations say they're estimates?

In a city built on request, stations come from OpenStreetMap, which often lacks station numbers, addresses and units, and first-due areas are estimated by drive time from each station. We've checked the stations of the cities on the list against public station listings, but your department's own list is still best. Send us your department's station list and we'll make them exact, or use Report a map problem in Profile for a single station.

How do I switch cities?

In Profile, under City. Each city keeps its own progress and home base, so switching back picks up where you left off.

The games

Which game should I start with?

Run today's Shift Drill: ten mixed questions show where you stand. Then take a Response Run from quarters and call the route turn by turn. Progress shows the streets you miss, and they come back until they stick.

Why was my route marked wrong?

Response Run accepts near-equal alternates, so a move counts as right when it keeps you close to the fastest route. Routes follow one-ways, turn costs (lefts cost more than rights) and signals, but they're a study model, not a dispatch router. If a route looks wrong for the real road, email us the address and the move, and we'll check it.

I can't hear the dispatcher or the tones.

On iPhone, the voice and sounds are muted when the ringer switch is set to silent. Flip it, turn the volume up (on Android, the media volume), and check that Read calls aloud (under Dispatcher voice) and Sounds are on in Profile. In Dispatch Drill you can also tap Show text to read the call.

How does the streak work?

Finish any game on a day to count it. Up to four days off in a row keep the streak alive, so studying on duty days of a 48/96 never breaks it.

The map and data

An address or street is missing.

Some addresses aren't in the open data yet, most often on newer streets. Where a street has no address points, its house numbers are estimated from public address ranges. To tell us about a missing or wrong street or station, use Report a map problem in Profile, or the flag on that street or station on the map. It goes straight to us, and we fix it in the next update. You can also email us the address. More about the map data.

Are fire hydrants on the map?

No. Hydrant locations aren't in the open data StreetDrill uses.

Is StreetDrill made by a city or fire department?

No. StreetDrill is an independent study aid made by Hantis USA, LLC. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any city or fire department. Always follow your department's SOGs, and verify routes before relying on them.

Your progress and data

Where is my progress saved?

On your phone, and nowhere else: StreetDrill has no account server. To move to a new phone, use Back up progress in Progress, then Restore from a backup on the new one. StreetDrill uses your location only to find your city when you ask and while Glance is open, and keeps only the city name. Read the privacy policy.

What's in the progress report?

Your name, Tester ID and role, the rating and notes you gave each game, your sessions, accuracy and weakest streets, and the questions you flagged. It goes out only when you tap Share progress report in Progress, as a text or email you send yourself.

How do I reset or delete my progress?

In Progress, tap Reset progress. That clears XP, your streak, game stats, mastery, history and ratings on that phone. Deleting the app removes everything. More about your data.

Can several people share one phone?

Yes, like a station iPad. Each person's progress is kept apart: the first screen lists everyone who has studied on it, and Profile › Switch person goes back to that list.
