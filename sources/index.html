Data sources

Updated November 4, 2026

StreetDrill's city maps are built from open and public data. Each city's full list of sources, with licenses, is in the app at the bottom of Profile, under Data sources.

Not a city or fire department app

StreetDrill is an independent study aid made by Hantis USA, LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any city, fire department or other agency. Map data can be incomplete or out of date: your department's maps, CAD and SOGs always come first.

Street data

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Streets, one-ways, signals, city limits and places come from OpenStreetMap.

Stations, districts and target hazards

Where we have a department's own station list or district map, StreetDrill uses it. In a city built on request, stations are drafted from OpenStreetMap and shown as estimates until a department's list is added. In November 2026 we checked the stations of the cities on the list against public station listings: missing stations were added, made-up station numbers were replaced with real ones, and closed stations were taken out.

Where a department's districts aren't available, first-due areas are estimated by drive time from each station. Target hazards (hospitals, schools, senior living, apartment complexes, venues and more) are drafted from OpenStreetMap.

How routes are built

Routes run on the street network with travel times by road class, turn costs (lefts cost more than rights), one-way rules, traffic signals, and U-turns only where the road allows them. They're a study model, not a dispatch router: they don't know about closures, traffic or your department's preferred routes.

What's not on the maps yet

  • Some addresses. Newer streets and addresses can be missing until the open data catches up. Where a street has no address points, its house numbers are estimated from public address ranges.
  • Fire hydrants. They aren't in the open data StreetDrill uses.
  • Stations in cities built on request. They're estimates from OpenStreetMap until a department's list is added.

Spot something wrong? Use Report a map problem in the app's Profile, or the flag on a station or street on the map. Have your department's station list? Email support@streetdrill.app.
